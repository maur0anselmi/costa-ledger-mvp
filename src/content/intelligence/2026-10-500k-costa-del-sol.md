Half a million euros can create real choice—but it also forces buyers to decide what matters most. A €500,000 budget can mean very different things depending on where someone searches on the Costa del Sol.

In one area, that amount may point toward a modern apartment in a newer development. Somewhere else, it may buy an established resale property. In a premium location, the buyer may need to accept less space in exchange for walkability or a stronger address.

That is why the budget only becomes meaningful once it is connected to the buyer’s priorities.

## Defining Your Lifestyle Priorities

A seasonal second-home buyer may care most about a terrace, parking, security and easy management. A retiree may prioritize healthcare access and year-round life. A family could care more about schools and daily convenience than being close to the most recognized luxury areas.

## New Build vs. Resale Dynamics

New property and resale can also produce completely different propositions:
- **New Construction:** May offer modern design and lower immediate maintenance, but the buyer still needs to investigate the developer, delivery stage, and future community costs.
- **Resale Homes:** Offers something different: the neighborhood, mature views, and the building already exist.

## The True Cost of Acquisition

There is also the question of how much of the €500,000 should actually go into the property.

A buyer with €500,000 available does not necessarily need to purchase a €500,000 home once acquisition costs, furnishing, and reserves are considered.

The better question is not whether €500,000 is enough. It is what combination of location, convenience, and lifestyle that amount can realistically buy.
