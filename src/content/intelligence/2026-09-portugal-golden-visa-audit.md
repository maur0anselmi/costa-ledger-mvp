Portugal’s Authorisation of Residence for Investment (ARI) program—widely known as the Golden Visa—remains a highly relevant residency tool for internationally mobile families. However, the program operates under fundamentally altered rules that cross-border capital must navigate with precision.

The single most critical adjustment: **direct real estate acquisition is strictly eliminated as a qualifying pathway.**

### Current AIMA-Approved Qualifying Routes

According to official regulations from the Agency for Integration, Migration and Asylum (AIMA), capital deployment is restricted to specific non-real-estate vehicles and economic contributions:

* **Venture Capital & Private Equity Funds:** Minimum **€500,000** subscription in non-real-estate collective investment vehicles.
* **Job Creation:** Establishment of a Portuguese corporate entity generating at least **10 full-time local positions** (or 8 in low-density territories).
* **Research & Scientific Investment:** Minimum **€500,000** allocated to accredited scientific research institutions.
* **Cultural Heritage & Arts:** Minimum **€250,000** supporting qualifying national cultural or artistic preservation projects.
* **Corporate Equity Injection:** Minimum **€500,000** into an existing Portuguese commercial business, paired with the creation or maintenance of at least 5 permanent jobs for 3 years.

### Fund Route Requirements & Structural Constraints

For international investors utilizing the popular **€500,000 fund subscription route**, AIMA enforces strict structural parameters:

1. **Jurisdiction:** The fund must be established under Portuguese law and regulated by the CMVM (Portuguese Securities Market Commission).
2. **Maturity Horizon:** The fund must hold a minimum maturity of at least **5 years** at the time of investment.
3. **Capital Allocation:** At least **60%** of the fund's capital portfolio must be invested directly in commercial enterprises headquartered in Portugal.

### Residency Maintenance & Family Extension

The physical presence requirements under the ARI regime remain exceptionally light compared to traditional residency pathways:

* **Year 1:** Minimum physical presence of **7 days** in Portugal.
* **Subsequent 2-Year Periods:** Minimum physical presence of **14 days** per two-year renewal block.
* **Benefits:** Full family reunification rights, Schengen area mobility, and eligibility to apply for permanent residence or Portuguese nationality after 5 years (subject to separate legal language and compliance requirements).

### The Decoupling Principle: Residency vs. Investment Quality

The central vulnerability for high-net-worth investors (particularly from the US, UK, and Canada) is treating residency eligibility as an endorsement of investment quality. **A fund can meet 100% of AIMA requirements while remaining an inherently weak financial asset.**

Investors must perform independent underwriting across six key variables:

1. **Management Quality & Track Record:** Verification of the fund manager’s prior exit capability.
2. **Fee Structures:** Performance fees, management hurdles, and embedded subscription costs.
3. **Liquidity & Exit Horizons:** Real secondary market friction after the 5-year lockup period.
4. **Tax Compliance & FATCA/PFIC:** Impact of holding Portuguese funds for US taxpayers or UK non-doms.
5. **Governance & Custody:** Independent custodian oversight and asset valuation transparency.

**Costa Ledger Verdict:** Portugal’s Golden Visa remains an efficient residency mechanism, but visa approval should never substitute for institutional investment due diligence. Residency strategy and asset allocation must be analyzed independently.
